VaR backtest

Each day's one-day VaR forecast uses only the previous 250 returns. A breach is a day on which the loss was bigger than the forecast. The Kupiec test asks whether the breach count is consistent with the stated rate.

Limits to keep in mind

  • The ticker list is hand-typed and is not the S&P 500. Compared with Wikipedia's record of index membership, 412 of its 473 stocks are current members, 91 current members are missing from it, and Yahoo returned no data for 21 of its tickers. Survivorship bias remains and its size is not measured.
  • Prices are Yahoo Finance split- and dividend-adjusted closes, used as downloaded; they are not strictly point-in-time.
  • A 99% VaR from a 250-day window rests on about 2.5 days in the tail, so it is noisy; the per-ticker tests are not independent of each other.
  • The portfolio and forecasting tests use one train/test split and a short test period. No transaction costs are modelled.
  • The forecasting model shows no demonstrated skill on the test period. Nothing here recommends buying or selling any security.
